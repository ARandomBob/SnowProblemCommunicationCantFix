Email templates

These templates drive every generated email — the .eml files and the copy buttons. Edits save automatically. Placeholders fill in when emails are built: {team}, {count}, {total}, {groups}, {groupField}, {date}, {delta} (week-over-week, e.g. “▲3 new · ▼5 cleared”), {teamdelta} (same, per team), {stale}/{teamstale} (no-update warnings), {knownerrors} (Known Error list with workarounds). Bullets that come up empty are removed automatically. Lines starting with • become bullets.
